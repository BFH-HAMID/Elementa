aim: Determine the concentration of an acid by neutralising it with a standard base and locating the indicator endpoint.

theory: At the equivalence point, stoichiometric amounts of acid and base have reacted. For a 1:1 strong acid–strong base reaction, c₁V₁ = c₂V₂. The indicator changes colour close to the steep pH jump.
theory_bn: equivalence point-এ acid ও base stoichiometric পরিমাণে react করে। 1:1 strong acid–strong base reaction-এ c₁V₁ = c₂V₂; steep pH jump-এর কাছে indicator colour বদলায়।
procedure:
  - Rinse the burette with titrant and fill it above the zero mark.
  - Pipette a fixed acid volume into the flask and add two indicator drops.
  - Run base quickly at first, then dropwise near the colour change.
  - Swirl continuously and stop at the first persistent pale endpoint colour.
  - Repeat until concordant titres are obtained.
procedure_bn:
  - titrant দিয়ে burette rinse করে zero mark-এর ওপরে fill করুন।
  - নির্দিষ্ট acid volume pipette করে flask-এ নিন, দুই ফোঁটা indicator দিন।
  - শুরুতে base দ্রুত, colour change-এর কাছে dropwise দিন।
  - ক্রমাগত swirl করে প্রথম স্থায়ী হালকা endpoint colour-এ থামুন।
  - concordant titre পাওয়া পর্যন্ত পুনরাবৃত্তি করুন।

calculation: For a 1:1 reaction, c_acid = c_base × V_base / V_acid. Use the mean concordant titre and volumes in the same unit.

precautions:
  - Read the meniscus at eye level.
  - Add only a small amount of indicator.
  - Do not overshoot the endpoint; rinse down flask walls with distilled water if needed.

viva:
  - q: Why is the endpoint not exactly the equivalence point?
    a: An indicator changes over a pH range, so its visible endpoint is an approximation to the stoichiometric point.
    q_bn: endpoint ও equivalence point ঠিক এক নয় কেন?
    a_bn: indicator একটি pH range-এ colour বদলায়, তাই visible endpoint stoichiometric point-এর approximation।
  - q: Why are concordant titres repeated?
    a: Agreement between close readings reduces random reading error and supports a reliable mean.
    q_bn: concordant titre বারবার নেওয়া হয় কেন?
    a_bn: কাছাকাছি reading-এর agreement random error কমিয়ে reliable mean দেয়।
