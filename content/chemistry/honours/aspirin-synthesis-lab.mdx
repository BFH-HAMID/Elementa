procedure:
  - Work under instructor supervision in a fume hood with gloves and splash goggles; weigh a measured mass of salicylic acid.
  - Add an instructor-specified excess of acetic anhydride and a few drops of approved catalyst; warm gently in a water bath, never over a naked flame.
  - After reaction, allow to cool; cautiously add water as directed to consume excess anhydride and cool in an ice bath to crystallize.
  - Filter and wash crystals with a small amount of cold water; dry to constant mass.
  - Record mass and melting range; compare with a reference sample and calculate theoretical and percentage yield.
