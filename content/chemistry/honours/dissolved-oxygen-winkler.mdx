  - "Key step: Collect the water without bubbles, fix the oxygen at once with manganese sulfate and alkaline iodide, acidify, and titrate the released iodine with thiosulfate."

  - "Repeat: Repeat at least three independent trials, preserve raw readings, compute the result with units and compare the observed trend against the stated model."
