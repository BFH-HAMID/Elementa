### Worked example / প্রয়োগ

For pure water at its triple point C=1 and P=3, so F=0: temperature and pressure cannot independently vary while three phases coexist. বাংলা: এক-উপাদান তিন-দশায় স্বাধীনতা শূন্য।

### Assumptions / সীমা

Assumes equilibrium, independent constraints and nonreacting components. For a reacting system use the number of independent components after accounting for independent reactions; at fixed pressure the reduced rule is F=C−P+1.
