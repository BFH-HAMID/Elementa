key_points:
  - "Aim: Determine the vitamin C content of a sample by direct iodimetry with a standard iodine solution."
  - "Apparatus: fresh vitamin C extract, standard iodine, starch indicator, burette."
  - "Principle: Ascorbic acid reduces iodine to iodide in a 1:1 molar reaction."
  - "Relation to use: $n(\\text{ascorbic acid}) = n(I_{2}) = C_{I_{2}}V_{I_{2}}$."
  - "Key step: Prepare sample rapidly with a known dilution, titrate a measured aliquot with iodine to a stable faint blue and run a blank for sample colour."
  - "Record: sample mass or volume, dilution factor, iodine C (M), titre (mL), ascorbic acid (mg) for every trial, with units."
  - "Calculation: The titration gives $n(\\text{ascorbic acid}) = n(I_{2}) = C_{I_{2}}V_{I_{2}}$; multiply by $176.12\\ \\mathrm{g/mol}$ and the dilution factor to recover the original sample amount."
  - "Repeat: Repeat at least three independent trials, preserve raw readings, compute the result with units and compare the observed trend against the stated model."
  - "Take care: Do not consume laboratory extracts; iodine stains and irritates skin."
  - "Error sources: Ascorbate air oxidation, other reducing substances and sample colour interfere."
  - "Viva: What is the ascorbic acid to iodine mole ratio?"
