theory: "For steady incompressible flow, $A_{1}v_{1}=A_{2}v_{2}$. Along a streamline in an ideal fluid, $P+\\tfrac{1}{2}\\rho v^{2}+\\rho gh$ is constant. In a horizontal Venturi, the narrow section has higher speed and lower static pressure; real viscosity causes deviations."
theory_bn: "স্থির অসংকোচনীয় প্রবাহে $A_{1}v_{1}=A_{2}v_{2}$। আদর্শ প্রবাহে একই প্রবাহরেখা বরাবর $P+\\tfrac{1}{2}\\rho v^{2}+\\rho gh$ ধ্রুব। অনুভূমিক ভেঞ্চুরির সরু অংশে বেগ বেশি ও স্থিতি চাপ কম; বাস্তব সান্দ্রতা বিচ্যুতি ঘটায়।"
procedure:
  - Assemble the Venturi rig as instructed by the teacher and remove visible air bubbles from manometer tubes.
  - Start a gentle steady flow; wait until the manometer readings stop fluctuating strongly.
  - Record the cross-sectional areas and pressure-head readings at the wide and narrow taps.
  - Collect water at the outlet for a measured time to estimate the volume flow rate Q.
  - Repeat at two or three pump settings, then compare measured pressure differences with the ideal prediction.

viva:
  - q: Why is the pressure lower in the narrow horizontal section?
    a: Continuity makes the fluid faster there; Bernoulli’s relation then requires lower static pressure when height is unchanged.
    q_bn: অনুভূমিক নলের সরু অংশে চাপ কম কেন?

  - q: Why may measured pressure differ from the ideal prediction?
    a: Viscosity, turbulence, entrance losses and sensor reading uncertainty are not included in the ideal equation.
    q_bn: মাপা চাপ আদর্শ পূর্বাভাস থেকে আলাদা হতে পারে কেন?
