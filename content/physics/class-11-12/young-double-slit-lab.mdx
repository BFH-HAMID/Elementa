theory: "For slit separation d, screen distance D≫d and monochromatic coherent light of wavelength $\\lambda$, the small-angle path difference gives $\\beta =\\frac{\\lambda D}{d}$. Bright fringes are nearly equally spaced; measuring several intervals reduces the relative position-reading error."
theory_bn: "চিরের ব্যবধান d, পর্দার দূরত্ব D≫d এবং $\\lambda$ তরঙ্গদৈর্ঘ্যের একরঙা সুসঙ্গত আলোয় ক্ষুদ্র-কোণী পথ-পার্থক্য থেকে $\\beta =\\frac{\\lambda D}{d}$। উজ্জ্বল ঝালর প্রায় সমান ব্যবধানে থাকে; অনেক ব্যবধান মাপলে অবস্থান-পাঠের আপেক্ষিক ত্রুটি কমে।"
procedure:
  - Set the laser and slit slide on a stable bench; place a screen several metres away and measure D from the slit plane.
  - Align the beam so the two-slit pattern falls centrally on the screen; reduce room light if needed.
  - Mark the centre of a bright fringe and a bright fringe n orders away; measure the total separation x.
  - "Calculate $\\beta = x/n$. Repeat for several fringe spans and screen distances without changing the slit slide."
  - "Compare measured $\\beta$ with $\\frac{\\lambda D}{d}$, or determine $\\lambda$ from the measured slope of $\\beta$ versus D."
