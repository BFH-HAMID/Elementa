theory: "For a launch and landing at the same height, $T=\\frac{2u \\sin\\theta}{g}$ and $R=u \\cos\\theta \\cdot T=\\frac{u^{2}\\sin(2\\theta )}{g}$. At a fixed speed, complementary angles have equal ideal ranges and the maximum is at 45°; air drag and unequal heights alter the result."
theory_bn: "একই উচ্চতায় নিক্ষেপ ও পতনের ক্ষেত্রে $T=\\frac{2u \\sin\\theta}{g}$ এবং $R=u \\cos\\theta \\cdot T=\\frac{u^{2}\\sin(2\\theta )}{g}$। নির্দিষ্ট বেগে পরিপূরক কোণের আদর্শ পাল্লা সমান এবং ৪৫°-এ সর্বোচ্চ; বায়ুর বাধা ও উচ্চতার পার্থক্যে ফল বদলায়।"
procedure:
  - Secure the launcher on a clear, level range; place a catch tray in the landing zone.
  - Set one launch-speed setting and record it if a speed sensor is available.
  - Choose an angle, launch the ball without changing the setting, and mark the first landing point.
  - Measure horizontal distance from the vertical point below the muzzle to the landing mark; repeat at least three times.
  - Repeat for complementary angles such as 30° and 60°, then compare means and estimate the spread.
