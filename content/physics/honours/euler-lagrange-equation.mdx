### Worked example · স্প্রিং–ভর

For a one-dimensional ideal spring, 

$$
L = \tfrac{1}{2}m q̇^{2} - \tfrac{1}{2}kq^{2}
$$

. Hence 

$$
∂L/∂q̇ = mq̇
$$

 and 

$$
∂L/∂q = -kq
$$

; the equation becomes 

$$
mq̈ + kq = 0
$$

. বাংলায়: গতিশক্তি ও বিভবশক্তির পার্থক্য থেকে একই সরল ছন্দিত গতির সমীকরণ পাওয়া যায়।

**Scope · সীমা:** The elementary form assumes holonomic constraints and independent coordinates; dissipative friction is not generally represented by `T−V` alone. A coordinate such as angle has unit rad (dimensionless); the units of `∂L/∂q` depend on the coordinate chosen.
