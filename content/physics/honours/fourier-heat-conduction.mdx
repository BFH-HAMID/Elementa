### প্রয়োগ / Worked example

A 0.10 m slab with κ=0.50 W/(m K), area 2 m² and ΔT=20 K transfers 200 W in steady state. বাংলায়: 0.50×2×20/0.10=200 W।

### সীমাবদ্ধতা / Assumptions

The relation is phenomenological; use tensor conductivity in anisotropic solids. The slab formula assumes steady one-dimensional flow, constant κ and negligible contact losses.
