### প্রয়োগ / Worked example

For an ideal gas U=U(T) and an isentropic expansion, temperature falls as volume increases; therefore the left side is negative, consistent with the right side. বাংলায়: অ্যাডিয়াবেটিক প্রসারণে T কমে।

### সীমাবদ্ধতা / Assumptions

Assumes equilibrium, fixed composition, differentiable U and only P–V work; across a first-order phase boundary derivatives may be discontinuous.
