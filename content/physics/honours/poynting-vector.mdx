### Worked example / প্রয়োগ

For a plane vacuum wave B=E/c, instantaneous flux is E²/(μ₀c); a sinusoid with peak E₀ has average intensity E₀²/(2μ₀c). বাংলা: গড় নিতে sin²-এর গড় ১/২ ব্যবহার করুন।

### Assumptions / সীমা

The stated form is for vacuum; energy flux in material media requires care about constitutive relations, and instantaneous S can vary with position and time.
